# Research collection

Use at R05 to carry out the bounded, authorized reads in R03/R04. Check the current brief, assignments, restrictions, freshness and stop conditions before collecting.

Collect with the least side effect appropriate to the source: file/search/Git reads, document inspection, citation-preserving public or authorized remote reads, permitted exports, snapshots or transcript/media inspection. Do not run researched code, installers, package scripts, migrations or deployments. A required substantial experiment becomes a separate authorized work item; it is not a reason to build an unrequested research/testing harness.

Capture citation and custody data at the time of each read: source ID, question/task, source class and role, exact path/URL or safe restricted pointer, locator such as line/page/section/commit/log range, retrieval method, collector, collection time, source-declared date/version, raw or derived status and relevance. Preserve source bytes or an allowed stable snapshot when necessary and permitted; do not copy protected material beyond its allowed use. A downloaded document alone does not prove it was read completely.

Record provisional currentness without prematurely assigning truth: live read, current-at-collection, version-pinned, historical, undated or needs-refresh. Keep source-declared time separate from collection time. An authorized worker report remains derived until its evidence can be traced; account for every expected returned pass before claiming coverage.

For each planned item that yields no usable evidence, record inspected locations, terms, depth, time bounds and access limits. Classify no-evidence-found, unavailable, restricted, too-stale, out-of-scope, unsafe or needs-owner-access separately. New source IDs, aliases and paths become explicit source-map deltas. Material changes to scope or source authority return to R03/R04 instead of silently extending the investigation.

Produce `evidence-corpus/index.md` with collected pointers, question/task links, locators, dates, provisional labels and restrictions. Produce `collection-log.md` with attempted and completed reads, negative searches, source-map deltas, returned-work accounting, blocked sources and resume needs. Set `collection_coverage`, `source_map_deltas` and `returned_work_accounted`.

Advance when every required planned item and assigned pass is represented by collected evidence or a specific gap. A corpus containing gaps can advance for honest analysis; a missing essential source may require WaitingInput. Collection neither accepts claims nor establishes a durable answer.
