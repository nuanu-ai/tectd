# Research result verification

Use at R11 before declaring Research complete. Read the delivered verification-before-completion skill within native boundaries: fresh evidence of the requested research outcome matters; inventing software tests or a new audit harness does not verify an analytical conclusion.

Reconstruct the original R01 question, immutable completion contract and checkpoint criteria if present. Compare the synthesis against every material question, inclusion/exclusion, currentness requirement and sufficiency criterion. Check that the claimed result actually answers the task rather than merely listing sources or describing a promising approach.

Trace every consequential conclusion to current claim IDs, qualified evidence and exact source locators. Confirm quotations/paraphrases preserve meaning, quantifiers, conditions and exceptions. Test whether apparently independent sources share one origin. Recheck material inferences against their premises, scope and alternatives. State explicitly what verification was performed; a digest or method-read receipt only verifies identity/delivery.

Check that contradictory, negative, inaccessible, stale, restricted, rejected and deferred findings remain visible and constrain the result. Confirm an absence conclusion is limited to the actual searched scope. Verify a recommendation is supported by the question and evidence, not by an unstated preference. If crucial source freshness expired during the work, return to the proper collection/qualification phase.

Write `research-verification.md` with reviewed exact outputs, question coverage, claim-to-evidence checks, observed findings and their resolution, remaining limitations, terminal eligibility and next action. Set `trace_checked=true` and `completion_supported=true` only after performing those checks; set `unresolved_limits` explicitly, including a justified none.

Address a defect by returning to its owning R02–R10 phase. Do not patch the conclusion in this verification record while leaving its ledger stale. Waiting for a source or authority is unfinished; inconclusive is terminal only under the frozen contract and remains a limitation of the answer. Verify publication only in a separate Knowledge Change if it was actually requested; its absence does not block a completed research answer.
