# Research contract

Use at R01 of `slice.research` to establish what answer would complete this inquiry. Read the native inquiry boundary and any exact source checkpoint first.

Restate the user's actual question, intended consumer and decision or reusable understanding it should support. Separate that outcome from a preferred answer. Establish inclusion/exclusion boundaries, subject and environment, allowed source classes, authority, sensitivity, currentness requirements, time/context budget and stop conditions. A technical failure with unknown cause belongs to Debug Root Cause; a simple lookup does not require this large pipeline. Route a misclassified task without silently expanding Research into implementation or operations.

Read the parent intent, already accepted decisions and available relevant DK. Declare missing context precisely: an empty knowledge base is legitimate; an unknown essential question, inaccessible required source or unresolved authority is a real gap. Label historical, generated, memory-derived, restricted and contradictory material before it becomes a premise. Do not demand a durable object model or target storage lane merely to begin answering a question.

Define sufficiency in terms of observable answers and evidence appropriate to the question. State whether a supported negative answer resolves it. The immutable `allow_inconclusive` flag determines whether a bounded unresolved result may complete this run; never change that policy in final prose. Inconclusive does not establish feasibility, absence or success of the parent objective.

For checkpoint-driven work, preserve the exact producer output/basis, question and answer criteria. Distinguish clarification of those criteria from substitution of a different research question. The source Brainstorming remains waiting; its uncompleted status is not missing evidence.

Produce `research-brief.md` with question, purpose, topic level, source/checkpoint basis, scope/non-goals, authority/sensitivity, freshness/proof policy, required context and gaps, sufficiency criteria, budget, stop/resume conditions and intended handoff. Set `topic_level`, `allow_inconclusive`, `question`, `source_boundary`, `sufficiency_criteria` and `stop_conditions` fields consistently with the frozen inquiry.

Advance when the brief can drive answerable questions without inventing scope. `waiting_source` and `waiting_authority` name unfinished work and exact resume needs. This phase may inspect existing context; it does not claim collection, an answer, publication or an executed experiment.
