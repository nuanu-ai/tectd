# Research synthesis and recommendation

Use at R10 after R09 declares ready or contract-permitted bounded inconclusive. Read the current question set, claim ledger, source qualifications, conflicts, negative knowledge and sufficiency record. Only current non-stale outputs support this phase.

Lead with the answer to the actual research question. Organize findings around the user's questions and decision, not the order of collection. Every material factual conclusion links to claim IDs and through them exact evidence. Separate observed/source-reported facts from an inference; show the premises of any new inference and route a genuinely new factual claim back to R07. Do not invent certainty because the result needs to be usable.

Explain the practical consequence. When a comparison or recommendation was requested, state what you recommend, the evidence and priorities that justify it, which alternative is strongest, and what new information would change the recommendation. When no recommendation was requested, say so briefly rather than adding unsolicited implementation or business decisions.

Carry forward independently the confidence, source authority, freshness, applicability, use restrictions and residual uncertainty. Keep contradicted, historical/stale, restricted, source-only, weak, rejected and deferred material visible where it limits the answer. Preserve bounded negative findings and access failures. A conflict can be described or resolved by an already evidenced distinction; prose alone cannot reconcile it.

Produce `synthesis.md` with direct answers, supporting claim groups, patterns/implications, comparison and recommendation when requested, limitations, unresolved questions, negative findings, next evidence/action and optional reusable knowledge candidates. A candidate's supporting claims, scope, restrictions, freshness/maintenance needs and likely owner are enough here; do not make a full RDF object model or seed changeset a prerequisite to finishing the answer.

Set `answer_coverage`, `recommendation_or_not_requested` and `limitations`. Return to R07/R08/R09 if the answer needs unledgered claims, missing conflict classification or a stronger sufficiency basis. An inconclusive sufficiency disposition cannot turn into an answered result through confident wording. Publication and execution remain separate work.
