# Research sufficiency and targeted follow-up

Use at R09 before synthesis to decide whether the evidence can support the contracted answer and whether more work would materially change it. Read R01 criteria, the question/claim coverage, conflicts, negative searches and remaining budget.

For every material question state the supported answer, limits, unresolved alternative and whether the missing information could change the conclusion or recommendation. Distinguish an unresolved fact from a preference or authority decision that more searching cannot settle. Check source independence, currentness and scope rather than counting citations. Absence claims need a bounded inspected domain; failure to retrieve a source is a different outcome.

Compare the expected value of the next specific read with its cost and ability to change the answer. If useful, name exact question/gap, source route, decisive observation, authorized method, remaining budget and stop condition. Return to R03 for a changed source plan or R05 for a bounded collection already within it. Subsequent qualification, ledger and conflict review must be repeated through their current bindings. Do not bypass them by adding a new fact to synthesis.

Write `sufficiency.md` with question-to-evidence sufficiency, outstanding blockers, inspected negative scope, plausible alternative explanations, currentness limits, budget used/remaining, decision and reason. Set `disposition` to `ready`, `bounded_inconclusive`, `targeted_follow_up`, `waiting_source` or `waiting_authority`, and set `justification`.

Use `ready` when every required material target is supported or resolved, including when the contract is satisfied by a valid bounded negative answer. Successfully classifying a required target as unresolved does not make the overall result ready. While a specific authorized bounded read has useful information gain, use `targeted_follow_up`; use `waiting_source` or `waiting_authority` when the necessary source or authority has not arrived. At the terminal decision, after no further authorized bounded work with useful information gain remains, use `bounded_inconclusive` when any required material target remains unresolved and the immutable `allow_inconclusive` flag is true; the final result must remain inconclusive. It does not prove the parent's preferred conclusion. If `allow_inconclusive` is false, exhaustion alone cannot manufacture completion; record the exact blocker and next viable action.

Do not initiate an unrequested harness, repeat the same search without a new premise, or broaden the question to keep working. This phase is a judgment about sufficiency, not a quota to collect more documents. A targeted follow-up has an owner, expected information gain and finite stopping condition.
