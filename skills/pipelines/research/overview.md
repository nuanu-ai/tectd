# Standalone Research

Answer an explicit substantial research question through a bounded evidence plan, accountable collection, qualified sources, atomic claims, contradiction and negative-result analysis, sufficiency judgment and a usable verified conclusion. This is an operational Slice outcome; creating durable knowledge is separate work.

The run has twelve required ordered phases R01–R12. Phasewise is the default. Whole delivery is allowed for a bounded question with clear source/access limits and enough context; it preserves every phase output, exact dependency, waiting state and rework obligation. A small incidental read can remain inside another pipeline instead of opening Research.

The immutable inquiry declares topic level and whether bounded inconclusive may complete the run. `answered` and `negative_result` require supported question closure; `inconclusive` is honest only when allowed; `waiting_source` and `waiting_authority` remain unfinished. Source verification is distinct from semantic truth. Publication, implementation, experimentation and operational execution are never inferred from a completed research result.

Research may consume one exact `research_request` checkpoint from a waiting Deep Brainstorming B05. It goes through normal candidate review/open/begin, returns an exact result, and leaves acceptance and subsequent decision work with that original Brainstorming run. Old Research-to-Durable-Knowledge definitions remain available for their existing history and runs.
