# Deep Brainstorming source ledger

Native pipeline: `slice.deep-brainstorming`. Initial native version: `0.5.0-native.inquiry.1`. Ten universal semantic methods authored personally by the primary Astra for Tony's approved design on 2026-09-15. This is a new inquiry outcome, not a shortened Full Design to Execution or a combined Research/Promotion/Development pipeline.

## Sources and adaptation

Read sources are the retained [Superpowers brainstorming body](../../references/superpowers/brainstorming/SKILL.md), the [Tect design shaper](../full-design-to-execution/v1/tect-design-spec-shaper.step.md), and the 22 retained [Research source bodies](../research-to-durable-knowledge/v1/) with their native ledger. These sources contribute problem/context framing, material alternatives, trade-offs, source qualification, negative evidence and honest handoff. They do not supply the new pipeline's router, authority, terminal states or artifact ownership.

The new methods deliberately generalize beyond software: a Program-level business choice, organizational model, marketing decision, QA strategy or operations boundary can be the topic of a Slice. Existing software component interrogation/cross-cutting/reconciliation/synthesis are not injected wholesale. Their useful principles—source fidelity, concrete findings, accountable reconciliation and preserved conditions—are expressed within the decision methods themselves.

Superpowers brainstorming's blanket trigger for every creative change, fixed option quota, mandatory section-by-section approval, visual-companion offer, external document location/commit rule and mandatory writing-plans terminal router are not imported. Those mechanics conflict with an already selected native inquiry and Tony's requested autonomy. Use actual missing information/authority, existing approval and a proportionate option space. No runtime skill outside the package is assumed to be installed.

## Behavioral invariants

The immutable inquiry controls topic abstraction and requested outcome. A recommendation completes a recommendation request; pending adoption cannot complete a decision request. The decision memo and final result agree with the exact current B08 disposition. Existing authority is reused without redundant permission; choosing a direction does not authorize unrelated spending, deployment, implementation or publication.

The B05 research_request is an operational checkpoint with exact producer definition/phase/output/basis, question and answer criteria. Normal reviewed Research can start while the producer waits. Acceptance of the exact returned result resumes the same B05; new facts may invalidate B02–B04 and successors through ordinary rework. Type-restricted direction prevents recursive research waits, and the candidate dependency DAG cannot fabricate readiness from an unfinished producer.

Phasewise is the default; whole preserves all ten ordered outputs, exact bindings and real waits. Root methods produce a primary decision memo with supporting versioned records, not a second manual lifecycle. Publication goes through Knowledge Change. No meaningful method duty is removed solely to reduce phase count.
