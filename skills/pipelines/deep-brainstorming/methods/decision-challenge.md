# Challenge and reconcile the decision

Use at B07 to check the reasoning against the original task and strongest counterexamples. The primary agent performs this substantive review under the applicable policy. A self-review must not be described as an independent reviewer verdict.

Reconstruct the required outcome and constraints from their original sources, then compare the current framing, criteria, options, evidence and recommendation. Look for an omitted stakeholder/constraint, changed scope, circular premise, favored-option bias, weak alternative, incompatible decisions, inappropriate abstraction, unsupported feasibility or missing effect that could reverse the choice. Test the recommendation under the most plausible contrary assumption and against doing less or declining when relevant.

Record findings as exact defects, with affected output/option/criterion, source basis, consequence, severity for this decision and owning phase. Do not create generic speculative audits or demand every software-spec artifact for a business choice. An unresolved preference is an owner question, not a factual defect that more research automatically fixes.

Reconcile findings deliberately. Correct a defect through allowed backward rework to its owner B01–B06 and regenerate dependent outputs. If an issue is already resolved by current evidence, show the exact basis. Preserve a genuinely non-blocking limit with its consequence and acceptance authority; do not silently waive an original requirement. If there are no material findings, record the scope actually checked rather than inventing work to make the phase look substantial.

Write `decision-challenge.md` with reviewed exact basis, concrete challenge cases, findings/dispositions, rework/resolution evidence, remaining limits and outstanding decisions. Set `findings_disposition`, `reviewed_basis` and `unresolved_decisions`.

Proceed to disposition only when the result is coherent enough for the requested choice or recommendation and unresolved conditions are visible. A finding that changes the selected objective needs explicit revision; a pending owner choice remains pending. Do not repair only the review note while leaving inconsistent earlier outputs active.
