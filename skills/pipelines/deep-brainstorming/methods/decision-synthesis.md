# Decision synthesis

Use at B09 to make the reviewed disposition understandable and reusable. Read the exact current B01–B08 outputs; compile them without inventing new choices or adding unsupported facts.

Lead with what was selected, recommended or rejected and why it serves the original objective. Explain the problem, relevant context, binding constraints, serious alternatives and decisive trade-offs in a connected memo. Include the strongest rejected alternative and the actual reason for rejection; do not turn past exploration into a claim that all other options were obviously wrong.

Preserve the distinction among verified fact, inference, assumption, preference and owner decision. Carry conditions, exceptions, negative findings, uncertainty and source/currentness limits to the place where they affect the conclusion. Identify what future observation or changed priority would justify revisiting the decision.

Explain consequences for the relevant Program/Scope/Slice without expanding the task. Name the next independently useful outcomes, their prerequisites and likely pipeline only when that helps continuation. Development defaults to Lightweight; Full requires actual indivisible complexity and a reason further vertical decomposition is inadequate. Research, Promotion and operations remain separately qualified work, not a mandatory combined chain.

Write `decision-memo.md` with disposition, chosen/proposed direction, rationale, alternatives, source/decision trace, conditions, consequences, unresolved limitations, next outcomes and revisit triggers. Set `disposition`, `rationale`, `alternatives`, `consequences` and `open_conditions`. Disposition must match the current B08 output and its authority.

Return to B07/B08 if compiling the memo exposes an inconsistent finding or unsupported decision. Do not smooth a pending choice into a selected one or create software interrogation/implementation artifacts for an unrelated strategic question. The memo is operational; publication requires Knowledge Change.
