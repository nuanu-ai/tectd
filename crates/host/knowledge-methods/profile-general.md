# General knowledge: meaning, evidence and lifecycle

This method is inherited by every profile. It applies to one operation against its exact baseline and source set. Read the delivered branch obligations and all specialized methods together; a specialized profile cannot weaken this one. The Knowledge Change backend supplies identity, versions, access checks and publication. The agent supplies evidence-led semantic judgment. Do not claim that schema conformance proves the claim.

At evidence qualification, identify each normative statement, decision, observation, hypothesis and negative result. Preserve its source, authority, scope, conditions, exceptions and uncertainty. A source snapshot is evidence of what the source says, not proof that it is correct or current. For observations preserve when, where and how they were obtained. Distinguish missing evidence from evidence of absence. A hypothesis may inform reasoning but must not become a mandatory execution constraint. Decisions record rationale and material alternatives; disagreement with a decision is not permission to rewrite it.

Create: resolve whether this is a new identity, an existing exact match, a revision or a conflicting claim. A similar title or vector match is only a candidate. Produce the smallest complete semantic unit with its source pins, kind, epistemic state, applicability and owner. Preserve material clauses even if they are long. Do not create separate conflicting copies for different profiles of the same unit.

Revise: compare the accepted baseline with the proposed meaning clause by clause. State additions, removals, changes of modality, conditions, exceptions, applicability, authority and epistemic state. Preserve the old revision and provenance. Reactivation of withdrawn knowledge requires an explicit reason, suitable current evidence and new review; it is not an incidental consequence of editing the title. A profile or type change is a reviewed semantic change, not a way to drop checks.

Revalidate: retain the same meaning and collect new evidence adequate for the original claim and the intended validity interval. A newer timestamp, repeated old source or passing structural check is insufficient. Record what was rechecked, against which current source/environment and with which limitations. If meaning, authority, applicability or conditions changed, return to planning for revise or another operation. Review overdue is not itself falsity; expired validity still prevents current use when required.

Supersede: name the eligible existing successor or the related create operation in this Change. Compare replacement coverage, material conditions and exceptions. Enumerate the exact existing bindings covered by replacement. Prevent self-reference, cycles and a successor that is itself unavailable. Partial replacement leaves uncovered bindings attached to the original knowledge; it does not silently rewrite pinned consumers. A replacement recommendation and an applied consumer change are separate results.

Retract: establish exact identity, authority, reason, scope and the effect on current consumers. Withdrawal prevents current delivery but preserves historical content/provenance under its access policy. Do not require the invalid old content to pass create checks before withdrawing it. Do not interpret withdrawal as deletion or as proof that dependent work has been repaired.

Erase: identify precisely the owned payload and copies to remove, shared-source boundaries and retained tombstone fields. Inventory canonical revisions, sources, proposals/baselines, outputs, receipts, manifests, artifacts and derived copies. Delete or redact whole owned containing records when safe fine-grained ownership is unavailable; never guess by substring. A shared independent source or independently authored claim needs separate scope. Unknown ownership, unresolved retention requirements or unauthorized scope blocks the affected erasure. Distinguish logical live-data removal, suppression on restore and physical storage/backup expiry; absence from a query is not proof of all three.

At impact planning, reconcile backend-discovered bindings and dependencies with semantic consequences. New knowledge can affect contexts that previously selected nothing. Record current-use gaps, unresolved contradictions, replacement suggestions and their owners. Access restrictions apply to read, history, exact replay and derived delivery. Never put credentials or secret values into a source snapshot merely to document them.

For every required obligation provide exact source/output references and a substantive conclusion. Reuse requires matching meaning, inputs, method/policy versions, authority and freshness; otherwise recheck. Ready means all current obligations and findings are reconciled. No-change needs an exact currently eligible existing result. Publication, impact settlement, erasure and the user's outcome are separately evidenced in the final receipt.
