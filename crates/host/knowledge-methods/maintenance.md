# Knowledge maintenance: exact cause, review and completion

Use the current backend actions and typed schemas to read, observe or resume maintenance work. A task records a specific reason and basis: review due, changed source, changed dependency, failed application or explicit owner request. Read its exact unit/revision, trigger evidence, current status and affected consumers before deciding what must be reconsidered. An external observation is a signal requiring review, not proof of a new canonical fact.

The backend deduplicates repeated signals and handles bounded mechanical retries, leases and recovery. It does not assess meaning, execute a runbook, perform an external probe or declare a unit fresh by timer. Preserve the task-to-Change link and supplied basis. Resume the existing Knowledge Change and its actual cursor; do not create another semantic workflow or a new Slice merely to reproduce its phases.

For semantic revalidation, obtain the new relevant evidence and use the existing domain profiles, source qualification, diff, review and publisher gates. Reuse an unchanged content revision only when the meaning remains valid. If meaning changes, use the normal explicit revise/supersede route; if evidence is insufficient, keep the precise waiting state. Previous sources can establish baseline but do not become new evidence because they were copied into a new task.

Read current actor and operation authority. A maintenance task authorizes neither a live infrastructure change nor publication outside the reviewed Change. Required consumer gaps remain visible until their actual cause is resolved. A reference warning does not become an execution permit. At Program level describe the strategic consequence; at Scope level the boundary/dependency impact; at a concrete execution phase the applicable operational detail.

Finish with the backend-verified publisher/validation outcome for the exact task basis. A completed conversation, a `done` string or an unrelated successful Change is insufficient. Newer signals remain open unless explicitly covered. Technical exhaustion names the failure and the needed changed condition; it is not a reason for unbounded retries or a new audit harness. Once that condition is addressed, an authorized owner may explicitly hand the same exhausted task to Knowledge Change through its current maintenance-begin action. The exact basis is checked again and the attempt counter is preserved; this does not restart the worker retry loop. Retraction/erase and restored database identity take priority over resuming old work; never reconstruct removed payload from a stale task or cache.
