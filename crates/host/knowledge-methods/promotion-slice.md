# Promotion to Durable Knowledge

Choose this Slice when the explicitly requested bounded outcome is to create or change reusable durable knowledge from already available material. The operation may create, revise, revalidate, supersede, retract or erase knowledge. Missing research or a substantial unresolved design question is an explicit dependency or a separately planned producer; it is not silently folded into promotion.

The opened Slice owns one Knowledge Change run. Begin it through knowledge.change_begin with the backend-provided exact PromotionSlice owner binding. Continue the same twelve Knowledge Change phases and read their exact composed methods. Do not also start slice.pipeline.begin or create a second cursor. The dedicated Change performs canonical publication and effect settlement; the Slice does not wrap a duplicate lifecycle around it.

The result is assembled from the actual Change outcome and publisher/effect receipts. Complete only when the original bounded outcome and required effects are satisfied; exact no-change may complete when the already eligible result truly satisfies intent. Rejected, waiting, blocked or partially settled knowledge is not a completed promotion. The backend records the managed Slice Result and advances planning input once; an agent-authored promoted flag or legacy slice.result.record cannot bypass this boundary.

Publishing a runbook or a security control does not execute it or verify its runtime behavior. A promotion also does not activate a skill, update vectors that are not configured, grant operational permission or perform new research. Use the domain methods to preserve these proof and authority distinctions.
