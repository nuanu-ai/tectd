# Research and Procedure Capture: exact handoff to Knowledge Change

Preserve the producer's research/procedure work, source boundaries, exact artifacts, proof limits and all existing phase skills. A candidate or approved promotion gate is not a canonical publication. When publication is within current task authority, hand off exact immutable producer outputs to a workspace Knowledge Change or an independently planned Promotion Slice. Existing authority can be sufficient; do not ask for it again merely because the writer is a separate backend owner.

The handoff identifies the intended units/claims, exact source outputs and artifact digests, applicability, proposed domains/operations, current authority and required returned evidence. The Knowledge Change independently resolves baseline, qualifies evidence and profiles, reviews the proposal, then publishes through its own canonical boundary. A paused producer may resume from that exact receipt; Knowledge Change must not wait for completion of the same producer's final result, which would create a dependency cycle.

For Research, promoted_to_durable_kb or promoted_with_restrictions requires a backend-resolved publisher receipt covering the stated units and exact contributing producer outputs. For Procedure Capture, procedure_promoted_after_approval requires the same proof for the applicable procedure/runbook units. An unrelated successful Change, arbitrary nonempty evidence text, foreign workspace receipt or stale/erased source does not qualify. The backend checks receipt identity/digest, covered operations, source lineage and current availability; the agent reviews whether the returned meaning satisfies the requested claims and restrictions.

For these promoted verdicts, copy the returned publisher receipt into the typed publication reference and bind the retained descriptive fields exactly: external_promotion_owner is `knowledge.change`; external_promotion_reference is the receipt ID as its canonical lowercase hyphenated UUID; external_promotion_digest is the receipt digest; external_promotion_authority_evidence is `sealed-command:` followed by the receipt's sealed_command_digest. The last field identifies the reviewed command containing the applicable authority basis; it does not grant permission. Use the backend values without inventing or recomputing hashes. Research's external_promotion_evidence remains a substantive account of what the referenced publication proves, including restrictions. Non-promoted verdicts retain their existing `none` and handoff requirements.

Producer fields durable_write_performed=false, durable_write_performed_by_this_slice=false, procedure_execution_performed_by_this_slice=false and skill_activation_performed_by_this_slice=false retain their ownership meaning. The separate publisher receipt proves the actual write; do not change those fields to pretend the producer performed it. Unavailable publication evidence leads to proposed, restricted, rejected, blocked or exact handoff truth according to the existing phase contract.

The index/front-door check distinguishes exact knowledge delivery from optional search readiness. In DK-2 search is not_configured, not an invented success or an endless worker wait. A task requiring unavailable search readiness cannot be called achieved. Maintenance and skill activation remain separately authorized follow-ups; this integration performs neither.

The new definition version requires this integration evidence. Existing runs continue with their pinned old definition and immutable history; do not silently retrofit their outputs or declare earlier external evidence backend-verified.

Source: design §7.10, §7.15, §13.7–13.8, §14 DK-2; existing Research 22-phase and Procedure Capture 17-phase definitions at version0.1.0-native.1.
